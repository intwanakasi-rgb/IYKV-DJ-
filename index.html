CONTROLLER MAPPING & CONNECTION

Click Connect controller, then move a control. No MIDI messages received yet.

Connect by USB and allow MIDI. FLX2: Play/Cue, EQ, pitch, channel faders, crossfader, filter, PFL, jog seek and hot cues. SHIFT + SMART FADER toggles effects; choose the effect on screen, then turn CFX (centre = dry). With FX off, CFX controls the filter. Enable VINYL for touch scratching. SLIP returns to the running timeline after a scratch, hot cue or loop. Switch deck pair here for C/D.
